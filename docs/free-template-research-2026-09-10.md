# Free PPT template research — 2026-09-10

## Method and originality boundary

Each source page below was opened and checked on 2026-09-10. Every selected page described a free template or free-use entry and supported Microsoft PowerPoint directly or a PowerPoint export. Research was limited to page-level descriptions of broad palette, contrast, information density, tone, and intended presentation scenario.

No source PPTX, POTX, Google Slides file, image, preview screenshot, illustration, icon, font, decorative asset, chart, map, table, text, data, page order, or recognizable layout was downloaded, imported, traced, or included in this repository. Every FreeAIPPT deck starts from a blank 16:9 canvas and uses only original English placeholder copy, system fonts, and editable PowerPoint-native shapes.
