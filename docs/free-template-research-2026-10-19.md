# FreeAIPPT template research — 2026-10-19

These public pages were opened and checked on the research date. Each page offered a free PowerPoint, Google Slides, Canva, or editable-template route appropriate to its category. They were used only to study broad color families, contrast, information density, hierarchy, mood, and presentation rhythm.

No source presentation files, screenshots, preview images, text, data, fonts, icons, illustrations, charts, maps, decorations, layouts, or page sequences were downloaded, imported, traced, or copied. Every FreeAIPPT deck below was independently authored on a blank 16:9 canvas with PptxGenJS and editable native PowerPoint shapes.
