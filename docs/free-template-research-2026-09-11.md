# Free PPT template research — 2026-09-11

## Research and originality method

- Research date: 2026-09-11 (UTC).
- Each reference page below was opened and checked for a currently visible free-use or free-download entry and PowerPoint support.
- No source PPTX, POTX, Google Slides file, image, preview, icon, font, illustration, chart, or decorative asset was downloaded or added to this repository.
- References were used only to observe broad color families, information density, tone, and presentation context. Every FreeAIPPT deck starts on a blank 16:9 canvas and is generated with system fonts and editable PowerPoint-native shapes.
- The resulting FreeAIPPT templates are independent original works. They are not modified, licensed, or derivative versions of the reference templates.
