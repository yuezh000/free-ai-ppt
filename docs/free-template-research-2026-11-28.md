## Originality

Each FreeAIPPT deck starts from a blank 16:9 canvas with editable native PowerPoint shapes and original English copy. Source files, slides, previews, screenshots, exact layouts, slide sequences, text, data, diagrams, charts, photographs, illustrations, icons, fonts, textures and decorations were excluded. The production, pilot, campaign, sales and classroom numbers are illustrative and visibly marked. The catalog and earlier research records were checked for repeated references, names and scenarios.
